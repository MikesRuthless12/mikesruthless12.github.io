Honesty

These are hand-authored parameters, not training data. Nobody's audio was analysed to build any of this. Each entry is a set of numbers a human wrote down from published research — tempo ranges, swing, which beats a kick lands on — and the names are used the way a genre name is used, to say what a style sounds like. There is no model trained on anyone here, and the plugin generates original patterns rather than reproducing any recording.
