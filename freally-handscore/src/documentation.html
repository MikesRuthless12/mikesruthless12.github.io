This manual grows with every version. Right now it covers what version 0.2.0 does.

Getting started

Handscore runs inside your DAW as a CLAP, VST3 or AU instrument, or on its own as a standalone app. Put it on an instrument track.

There are no downloads before version 1.0.0, and the source code is private.

The licence agreement

The first time Handscore opens, it shows its licence agreement. Until you choose Agree, Handscore does nothing and makes no sound.

Your answer is kept on your computer and shared by every open copy of Handscore, in every DAW and in the standalone app. If you decline, the agreement stays on screen, ready for whenever you agree.

The stage

Below the top bar is the stage: the piano you chose, its 88 keys, A0 to C8, at a real piano's measurements, filling the width of the window. Every key's name is on its front — white keys in dark ink, black keys in white — and hides while the key is down, so you can always see which notes are being played.

If your computer cannot draw in 3D, the stage says so and explains how to turn it on, instead of showing nothing.

Handscore measures how fast your computer draws the stage and settles on the quality it can hold smoothly.

The pianos

Four pianos take the stage one at a time: Grand Piano, Acoustic Piano, Upright Piano and Honky-Tonk Piano, each with its own shape, finish and sound.

The instrument button at the top left (or P) opens the piano picker. Preview plays a short phrase on a piano without switching; Choose puts it on the stage, crossfading from the last one. Your choice is saved with your project.

Playing the keys

Click a key to hear it, or slide across the keys to play each in turn. Each key goes down on its balance rail, faster the harder it is played, and its name fades while it is down and comes back when it rises.

MIDI from your DAW plays the piano and moves its keys. The sustain pedal holds the notes you let go until you lift it.

Light and dark

The Dark theme switch at the top right turns the window light or dark. Handscore remembers your choice, and so do the other Freally apps on your computer.

Languages

Handscore speaks eighteen languages and starts in your system's language.

Your privacy

The plugin and the standalone app never use the network. If Handscore ever stops unexpectedly, a short report is kept on your computer and offered the next time it opens; nothing is sent unless you choose to report it.
