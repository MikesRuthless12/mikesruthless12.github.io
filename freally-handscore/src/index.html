Watch real hands play your music. Then drag it into your DAW. Chords, a melody, a bassline and a countermelody, written in any style, shown as four sheets of music and played on a realistic piano by realistic hands. Offline and 100% AI-free.

What it is

Most producers who use a chord generator never see how its parts would be played. Handscore shows you.

Pick a genre, an artist or a producer and Handscore writes four parts in that style: chords, a melody, a bassline and a countermelody. They appear as four sheets of music on the piano's music desk, and a pair of realistic hands plays them on a realistic piano, every finger on the key a pianist would use.

Each key carries its name on its front, so you can see exactly which notes are being played. When a part is right, drag it from its sheet straight onto a track in your DAW.

What it will do

Real pianos

A grand, an acoustic, an upright and a honky-tonk piano, played on the same measured piano voices as Freally MIDI Master.

Realistic hands

Two hands that play every note with the finger a pianist would choose, pressing each key down as it sounds.

Four sheets of music

Chords, melody, countermelody and bass, each on its own sheet, with every note lit as it plays.

Every style Freally MIDI Master knows

73 genres, 1,507 artists and 833 producers, from the same rule-based generators.

Drag the MIDI

One part at a time or all four together, dragged from the sheets straight into your DAW. That is the only way MIDI leaves Handscore.

Front, overhead and side views

Switch between them while the music plays; the sheets turn to stay readable and the hands never miss a note.

In any DAW

CLAP, VST3 and AU, plus a standalone app. Windows, macOS and Linux.

Offline, no AI

No account, no telemetry, no network, no model. Every part, fingering and movement comes from rules, enforced by a build gate rather than promised.

The road to 1.0, and on to 2.0

One line per version. After 1.0, each version brings a new family of instruments, played by realistic hands the way a teacher would show you.

A version that is done is built and tagged. It is not a download: there are none before 1.0.0.

Where it is today

It is at its very beginning

Version 0.2.0 loads in a DAW and opens on four real pianos, every key named, that you play with the mouse or from your DAW. The hands and the sheets arrive version by version, as the ladder above shows.

There are no downloads before 1.0.0

No beta on this page, no preview build and no early access.

It never goes online

The plugin and the standalone app contain no networking code, and a build gate fails if any is added. A later version may add an update check that stays off unless you switch it on, run by a separate program.
