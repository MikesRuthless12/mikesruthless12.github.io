Handscore is free today: no paid tier, no subscription and no account. There is nothing to download yet, because there are no downloads before version 1.0.0, and later versions may be sold.

What version 1.0 does

Four real pianos

A grand, an acoustic, an upright and a honky-tonk piano, each with its keys moving as they sound, and played on the same measured piano voices as Freally MIDI Master.

Guitars and the bass guitar

Acoustic, nylon, electric and muted guitars, fretted, picked and strummed for real, and the bass guitar, played with the fingers and slapped.

Realistic hands

Two hands that play every note the way a player would: the finger a pianist would choose, the fret and the stroke a guitarist would use.

Four sheets of music

Chords, melody, countermelody and bass, each on its own sheet, with every note lit as it plays.

Every style Freally MIDI Master knows

Pick one of 73 genres, 1,507 artists or 833 producers, and the same rule-based generators write the four parts in that style.

Your own audio and MIDI

Bring in your own audio or MIDI, and Handscore reads it into parts for the hands to play.

The classics

Public-domain songs to learn, from nursery songs and carols to anthems and classical themes, each arranged for two hands and played by the hands on the stage. Only their chords can ever leave Handscore.

Music lessons

A piano course from Day 1 that listens as you play and moves on when you have it right, then courses in reading music, rhythm, ear training, theory, songwriting and lyrics, song arrangement, mixing, finger drumming and the drum kit. Switch Read Aloud on and each lesson is read to you, each word lit as it is spoken. Switch Listen on and the lessons hear a real instrument or your voice through a microphone or a cable, on this computer only.

Drum machine and drum kit

A drum machine with your own samples on its pads, each pad's sound shaped and its steps programmed, and a professional's drum kit played by a drummer's hands and feet, one switch between them. A pad controller played by realistic hands, every pad lit as it is hit. Generate a drum pattern, play your own in or bring in your DAW's drum MIDI, and learn finger drumming and the drum kit from the first beat to full grooves.

Whole songs and a mixer

Build a song from sections by hand or start from an artist's arrangement, then mix every part on its own strip, and drag it out with the mix kept.

Drag the MIDI

One part at a time or all four together, dragged from the sheets straight into your DAW, or saved as a MIDI file or as a PDF of the sheets.

Front, overhead and side views

Switch between them while the music plays; the sheets turn to stay readable and the hands never miss a note.

In any DAW

CLAP and VST3 on Windows, macOS and Linux, AU on macOS, and a standalone app for working without a DAW.

Offline, no AI

No account, no telemetry, no network and no model. Every part, fingering and movement comes from rules.

The short version

Of these four, only Handscore 1.0 has every row: parts written in named styles, realistic hands playing them, the sheet music, the drag into your DAW and the plugin.

Where they win

Scaler 3 is a much broader music-theory workstation. Synthesia is made for learning pieces on your own keyboard; Handscore's lessons listen as you play from version 0.6, but playing along live waits for version 1.9. AR Pianist puts its pianist in your room in augmented reality, which Handscore does not do.

Scaler, AR Pianist and Synthesia are the trademarks of their owners, who are not affiliated with Freally Handscore. Tell us if a cell is wrong and we will correct it.

Why it is built the way it is

Rules, not a model

Every part, every fingering and every movement comes from rules, with no model anywhere. The same style and the same seed give the same parts, every time.

What you see is what you drag

The sheets are drawn from the MIDI they drag, and the hands play exactly those notes, so what lands on your track is what you watched.

Built to real measurements

Every instrument is built to its real measurements: the keyboard is a real piano's size, and every key turns on its balance rail the way a real key does.

It stays on your machine

No account, no telemetry and no cloud. The plugin and the standalone app never go online, and a build gate fails if networking code is ever added.

What is still coming

Stated plainly, because a feature list that hides its gaps is not worth reading. This page describes version 1.0, and Handscore is built one version at a time, so most of it is not built yet. Each version on the road to 1.0 is marked with its state.

What 1.0 does not do

  • A song whose melody matches a classic or a well-known tune leaves Handscore as its chords only.
  • It plays the pianos, the pad controller and the drum kit, the guitars and the bass guitar. Every other family of instruments arrives one version at a time after 1.0, up to the whole band at 2.0.
  • Its lessons listen as you play, but the hands do not yet follow you live: playing along on any instrument arrives in version 1.9.

There are no downloads before version 1.0, and none on this page.
