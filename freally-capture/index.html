Freally Capture

Record and stream like a studio — scenes, sources, multistream, one clean app.

A live-streaming and recording studio for Windows, macOS and Linux. No accounts, no telemetry and no cloud service of ours: your stream goes straight to the platforms you choose.

What it is

Going live usually takes one app to capture, another to mix the sound, a service to send the stream to several platforms, and an account with each of them.

Freally Capture is one studio on your own computer. Build a scene from your screen, windows, cameras, media and overlays; compose it on the graphics card; record it on up to six audio tracks; and stream it to several platforms at once, straight from your machine with no relay in between.

It needs no account and sends no telemetry. What leaves your computer is the stream you chose to send, plus the few connections you switch on yourself — each listed plainly in the documentation.

What is in it

Scenes on the GPU

Build scenes from displays, windows, games, cameras, web pages, media, images and text, and compose them live on the graphics card with filters and transitions.

Multistream, direct

Go live on Twitch, YouTube, Kick, Facebook, Trovo or any server of your own — several at once — over RTMP, RTMPS, SRT or WHIP, straight from your machine.

Multi-track recording

Record with your graphics card's hardware encoder or losslessly in Capture's own format, on up to six audio tracks, with splitting, chapter markers and crash-safe files.

A broadcast mixer

Every source on its own strip, with noise suppression, a gate, a compressor, EQ and loudness metering — all classic signal processing, with no AI anywhere.

Studio Mode and safety

Stage changes in the preview before they go live, check everything with a pre-flight, and keep alarms, encoder failover and a panic button ready.

Remote guests

Invite up to eight guests with a link. They join from a web browser, and their camera and microphone come straight to you, peer to peer.

Virtual camera, browser source, game capture

Your program as a webcam for video calls, any web page as a source, and exclusive-fullscreen games — Windows first, with every gap stated.

Free today, local, no AI

No paid tier, no account, no telemetry and no model. Your scenes, recordings and stream keys stay on your computer.

The things most sites put at the bottom

Some of the newest features are Windows-only

In 1.2.1 the virtual camera, game capture and cursor effects work on Windows only, and the browser source has been verified on Windows first. The rest of the studio runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Two components are not code-signed yet

The virtual camera's system component and the browser source's runtime are not code-signed yet, and two live tests are still to be done: the camera inside a real video call, and game capture in a protected game. The changelog says so in the release that added them.

What does go online

Your streams go to the platforms you set up. A remote-guest session's handshake crosses a public signalling service; a chat overlay reads a channel's public chat; ffmpeg and the browser runtime are downloaded the first time you need them; the app checks for updates; and a problem report goes only when you press Send on its page. Apart from the update check, each happens only because you set it up.

There are no downloads here

This site describes Freally Capture; it does not distribute it. There is no download page, link or button, and the source code is private.
