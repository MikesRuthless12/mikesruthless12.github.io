Freally Unloop

Every loop is a record waiting to happen.

Turn an 8-bar loop into a finished, arranged record in the style of any artist, producer or genre — performed live in your DAW, then dragged out as audio stems or as a MIDI song template. Free, offline, and 100% AI-free.

CLAP · VST3 · AU · Standalone · Web app — Windows · macOS · Linux

In development — now at v0.6.0, the timeline: every clip, fade, fader, automation lane and section is yours to edit. There are no downloads on this site until v1.0.0, and the source code is private.

Freally Unloop is free today, but it might not be free forever: future versions may be sold.

How it works

  1. Loop it. Your 8 bars loop in the DAW — or drop stems or a MIDI loop into the standalone or the web app.
  2. Pick a name. Search 2,413 artists, producers and genres and choose a mode: every genre can be arranged several ways.
  3. Press play. Unloop performs the whole record over your own audio — intros, hooks, drop-outs, 808 cuts, stutter builds, tape stops, beat switches — in time with your project.
  4. Shape it. Move, copy, split and fade clips, ride faders, draw automation, lock what you like and reroll the rest.
  5. Take it. Drag out the arranged record as WAV, AIFF, FLAC or MP3 — mix or stems — or as a multi-track MIDI song template.

The charter

  • No AI. A deterministic, rule-based planner and classic signal processing — enforced by a build gate.
  • Free today. No tier, no trial, no account, no key, no ads — though future versions may be sold.
  • Offline. No telemetry and no network code in the plugin or the standalone.
  • Your sound, arranged — never replaced. Unloop never writes new music into your song, and never recreates a specific song's arrangement.
