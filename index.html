Desktop apps for producers and creators.

Eight apps for Windows, macOS and Linux: four for making music, and four for the work around it. They run on your own computer, look like one family and speak eighteen languages.

Free today. Every Freally app is free today, but it might not be free forever: future versions may be sold.

The apps

For making music

  • Freally MIDI Master Artist-accurate MIDI, generated in your DAW, in your project's own key and tempo.
  • Freally Flipcopy A copy machine for your clips: audio in, MIDI out; MIDI in, audio out.
  • Freally Oscillate A DAW where four producers work in the same session at once.
  • Freally Unloop Turns an 8-bar loop into a finished, arranged record.

For the work around it

  • Freally Sourcerer One search for every file, on every OS.
  • Freally File Manager Copy, move, sync and verify files, byte for byte.
  • Freally Teleprompt A teleprompter that reads at a real human pace.
  • Freally Capture Record and stream like a studio, straight from your machine.

About Freally

Freally Products makes desktop software for people who make things: producers, musicians, streamers, presenters, and anyone with a lot of files. Every app is built to do its one job well, on your own computer, without getting in your way.

The apps are made by Mike Weaver, and they share one design, one set of controls and one way of being honest about what they do.

  • On your computer

    Real desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux, not a website you rent.

  • Honest about what's done

    Each app's site says what works today, what doesn't yet, and where other tools do better.

  • Eighteen languages

    Every app and every site, in the same eighteen languages.

  • Free today

    Free today, with no paid tiers. Future versions may be sold, and the sites will say so first.
